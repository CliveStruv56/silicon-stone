# Deep Dive variant (canonical)

> Inherits all rules in [[house-style]]. This file adds only what is specific
> to the Deep Dive variant. Canonical — synced into the website repo, not edited there.

## Purpose

- Comprehensive, exhaustive examination of complex issues (Audit tier) that will compound over time.

## Length & structure

- **Target length:** `3,000–6,000 words`.
- **Structure:** Title, Subject Line, Preview Text, Methodology Audit (eligible for all 6 cells; claim only cells visibly applied), structured sections with clear navigation (headings), data visualizations/framework tables, and a Stone Truth Callout.

## Tone & depth

- Maximum analytical depth, eligible to apply all 6 cells of the 3×2 matrix. Heavy documentation, rigorous analysis, and strict adherence to evidence.

## Must include / must avoid

- **Must include**: Evidence-led Methodology Audit, Stone Truth Callout, and comprehensive primary citations.
- **Must avoid**: Superficial summaries, generalist commentary, or claiming analytical lanes the body does not visibly apply.

## Brand notes

- Silicon & Stone use: Foundational analysis of major regulatory/geopolitical shifts (e.g. complete sector compliance roadmaps or supply chain assessments).
- Waymark Path use: Detailed career transition playbooks, regional capability audits, or comprehensive skills roadmaps.
