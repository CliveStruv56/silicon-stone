# Pulse variant (canonical)

> Inherits all rules in [[house-style]]. This file adds only what is specific
> to the Pulse variant. Canonical — synced into the website repo, not edited there.

## Purpose

- Quick read of an emerging signal, where speed matters and depth is constrained by what is actually known. Used for breaking signals.

## Length & structure

- **Target length:** `100–140 words` (a genuine 30-second scan).
- **Structure:** Snappy title, subject line, preview text, and a single-paragraph body stating what shifted, why it matters, and a single watchpoint.

## Tone & depth

- Concise, high-level summary with minimum analytical depth. Focuses on the core event and its immediate implication. Applies 1–2 cells of the 3×2 matrix.

## Must include / must avoid

- **Must include**: Above-the-fold metadata, a single watchpoint, and a single-sentence "Stone Truth" summary.
- **Must avoid**: Deep multi-faceted analysis, primary document investigations, or applying all 6 cells of the matrix (overkill).

## Brand notes

- Silicon & Stone use: Breaking regulatory/policy news and rapid supply chain chokepoint alerts.
- Waymark Path use: Rapid updates on AI career/skills impact, or urgent training-needs changes.
