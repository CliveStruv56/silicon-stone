# Edit Summary — Output Format

After the edited article, append a summary in this structure. Keep it tight —
it is a working hand-back, not a report.

-----

```markdown
## Voice Edit Summary

**Edited against:** [Silicon & Stone style guide / Waymark Path style guide /
defaults] · UK English

### AI tells removed
- Banned vocabulary: [N] instances ([e.g. "delve" ×2, "robust" ×3, "tapestry"])
- House banned words: [N] ([e.g. "unlock" ×2, "empower", "leverage" (verb)])
- Hedges cut: [N] ([brief examples])
- Structural tics fixed: [list — e.g. "broke three rule-of-three lists; removed
  two summarising kickers; converted one bullet list to argued prose"]
- Empty connectives removed: [N]

### House-style corrections
- UK English: [N fixes] · Smart quotes: [converted? Y/N]
- Em-dashes: [any paragraphs over the 3-per-para cap? fixed]
- Headlines/subheads: [rhetorical questions rewritten? exclamation marks removed?]
- Structural furniture: [Title/Subject/Preview/Article layout + Stone Truth
  callout preserved? Y/N]
- Regulatory (if applicable): [fact/inference/scenario labelled? Last reviewed
  date present?]
- Open TODOs flagged: [Oxford comma / numerals consistency notes, if relevant]

### Structural changes
- [Bullet per significant change — paragraph reorders, section cuts, rhythm
  changes, position-taking where the draft hedged]

### ⚠ Author specifics needed
These placeholders need a human detail before publishing — do not publish with
them in place:
- `[AUTHOR: …]` — [location in article] — [what's needed and why]
- `[AUTHOR: …]` — [location] — [what's needed]

### Verdict
[One or two sentences: is this ready once the placeholders are filled, or does it
need a structural rethink? Be honest.]
```

-----

## Notes on filling the summary

- **Count honestly.** If you cut twelve hedges, say twelve. The counts tell the
  author where the draft model is weakest so they can tune their drafting prompt.
- **The placeholders are the point.** This section is the most valuable part of
  the hand-back — it is the list of things that will make the article
  unmistakably the author's. Make each one specific enough to act on without
  re-reading the whole draft.
- **Be willing to say it's not ready.** If the draft is generic all the way down,
  the verdict should say so and recommend the author supply substance before any
  amount of editing will help.
