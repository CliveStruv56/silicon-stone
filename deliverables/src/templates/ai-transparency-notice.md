## How to use this template

*Delete this page before you publish the notice.*

This is a short public statement that tells people when they are dealing with AI, supporting the Article 50 transparency duties that have applied since 2 August 2026. Publish it where people will actually meet it — a footer link, a chatbot's opening message, a labelled banner on generated content — not only inside a privacy policy.

- Replace every **[bracketed field]**, which is highlighted in the Word file.
- Delete any bullet that does not apply to you rather than leaving an empty promise.
- Keep the notice and your Register in step: every system whose Register row lists Checklist B should appear here, and every system listed here should have a Register row.
- Do not claim human review you do not perform, and remove systems you have retired.

> A transparency notice is judged by accuracy, not polish.

The notice does not replace the disclosures Article 50 requires at the point of interaction itself (checklist rows B-02 and B-03) or the labelling of generated content (B-05 to B-07).
