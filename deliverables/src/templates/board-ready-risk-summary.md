## How to use this template

*Delete this page before you circulate the summary.*

Replace every **[bracketed field]** with figures from the **Dashboard** tab of your toolkit workbook, which calculates each count from the Register, Suppliers and Actions tabs. Keep it to a single printed page — the discipline is the point.

- **Position in one line:** use the Dashboard's figures, not an impression. Say plainly if anything is prohibited or unclassified.
- **Systems at a glance:** the rows match the Dashboard's AI systems block. "Not yet classified" belongs on the page; a board should know what has not been looked at.
- **Top gaps:** take them from the Assessment's critical findings and the Requirements tab rows marked "Applies — no action raised".
- **Vendor dependencies:** list High-band suppliers first, and any whose scoring is still incomplete.
- **Readiness** is the Assessment's measure of what you can show. It is not a compliance conclusion, and the page should not present it as one.

> The board does not need the regulation explained. It needs the size of the exposure, what is already covered, and what you are asking for. One page, three minutes.
