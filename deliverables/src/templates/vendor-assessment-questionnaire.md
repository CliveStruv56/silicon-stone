## How to use this template

*Delete this page before you send the questionnaire.*

Send this to a prospective or incumbent AI vendor **before you sign**, not after. The answers populate your Register, evidence your classification, and give you the facts to score the vendor on the Suppliers tab of your toolkit workbook.

- The **Informs** column shows which of the five dependency dimensions each question feeds: Data Sovereignty, Contractual Lock-In, Regulatory Risk, Concentration Risk and Alternative Availability. The workbook's Scoring key lists the same question numbers against each dimension.
- Record the date you sent it and the response status on the Suppliers tab, and file the reply at the evidence location you record there.
- A vendor that cannot or will not answer the scope, documentation and data-location questions has told you something material. Record the non-answer as a finding and score the dimension accordingly.
- The questionnaire is most useful comparatively: run two or three vendors through the same questions before deciding.

> Keep the question numbers as they are. The workbook's Scoring key refers to them.

# Vendor Assessment Questionnaire

**To:** [vendor legal entity] · **System or service:** [name] · **From:** [your organisation, contact name and email] · **Reply by:** [date]

We are assessing the AI systems we use against the EU AI Act and our own governance policy. Please answer each question in writing in the response column, attach supporting documents where you refer to them, and tell us plainly where you cannot answer. Your replies will be kept on file with our record of this system.
