## How to use this template

Replace every **[bracketed prompt]** with your own figures from the *AI Systems Inventory* and *Vendor Dependency Scorecard*. Keep it to a single page when printed — the discipline is the point. Delete this section before you circulate it.

> The board does not need the regulation explained. It needs to know the size of the exposure, what is already covered, and what you are asking for. One page, three minutes.
