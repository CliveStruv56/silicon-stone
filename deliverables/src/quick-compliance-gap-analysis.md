## How to use this

This is a structured first read, not a legal opinion. Work through the twenty questions in order. For each, mark **Yes**, **No**, or **Unsure**, then carry the points into the scoring grid at the end.

The questions are grouped into five short sections that mirror how the Act actually decides what you owe: first whether you are in scope and in what role, then whether anything you run is prohibited, then whether anything is high-risk, then your transparency duties, and finally whether you can prove any of it.

> A gap analysis is meant to create clarity, not anxiety. Most organisations score Amber on a first pass — real gaps, but addressable with structured effort over the months the staged timeline now allows.

**Before you start, one date correction.** The "August 2026 cliff" you may have read about no longer holds. The 2026 Digital Omnibus split the obligations across distinct dates:

| Date | What goes live |
| :---- | :---- |
| 2 February 2025 | Prohibited practices banned; AI-literacy duty (Art 4) |
| 2 August 2025 | General-purpose AI model rules |
| 2 August 2026 | Transparency duties (Art 50); penalties and governance framework |
| 2 December 2027 | Standalone high-risk systems (Annex III) |
| 2 August 2028 | Embedded / product-safety high-risk systems (Annex I) |

So the heavy high-risk obligations are a 2027–2028 programme, not an August 2026 emergency. That is breathing room to do this properly — not a reason to defer starting.

## Scoring

Score each answer: **Yes = 2 · Unsure = 1 · No = 0.** Add them up — maximum 40.

| Your total | Band | What it means |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 32–40 | **Green** | Broadly compliant. You have the structures; the work is upkeep, evidence, and the 2027 high-risk detail. |
| 16–31 | **Amber** | Significant but addressable gaps. You know roughly where you stand; you need a register, owners, and a plan against the staged dates. |
| 0–15 | **Red** | Substantial structural work. Start with cataloguing systems and confirming nothing prohibited is running, then build outward. |

> Two zero-scores deserve special weight whatever your total: any **Unsure** on Section B (prohibited practices) and any **No** on question 18 (no register). Prohibitions are already enforceable, and you cannot manage what you have not written down.
