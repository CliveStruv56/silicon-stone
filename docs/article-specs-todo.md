## C1 — CRA Briefing (P1)
**Abstract:** The CRA's reporting duties apply 11 Sept 2026 — ahead of most AI
Act high-risk obligations — and reach products already on the market. Why an AI
feature can't be decoupled from the secure-software duties of the product that
ships it.
**Sources:** Commission CRA summary; Reg (EU) 2024/2847.
