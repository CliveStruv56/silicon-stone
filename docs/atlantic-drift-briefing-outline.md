# Atlantic Drift Briefing — Outline

**Working title:** *5 Supply Chain Chokepoints Every European Executive Should Monitor in 2026*
**Format:** PDF, 10–15 pages, designed for executive distribution and email-gated download
**Role:** Flagship lead-magnet. The first PDF a reader sees, the artefact every YouTube video and homepage CTA points to.
**Status:** Outline v2 — open questions resolved, ready for drafting.
**Last updated:** 2026-05-14

---

## What this PDF has to do

Three jobs, in priority order:

1. **Convert email-curious readers into Kit subscribers** by being a credible enough deliverable that handing over an email address feels like a fair trade.
2. **Demonstrate the methodology** so that the reader leaves the PDF with an intuitive grasp of Forensic Technopolitics — five worked applications of the matrix, on five concrete chokepoints.
3. **Pre-sell the paid Intelligence Series** by doing a fraction of what the paid PDFs do at full depth, so the upgrade is visible.

Every design decision in the document should serve at least one of these three. Anything that doesn't, gets cut.

---

## Audience

Primary: **Industrial Ian** — supply chain and operations directors, particularly in European semiconductor, automotive, and industrial manufacturing.
Secondary: **Compliance Clara** (export control implications), **Sovereign Sofia** (strategic autonomy framing).
Tertiary: **Global Citizen** — informed professionals who want substance.

The PDF should work for an Ian first. The other personas pick up adjacent value.

```

  6-cell grid showing which cells were applied to this chokepoint.
```

#### Working candidate chokepoints

To be confirmed by Clive based on current Inoreader signal density and Pinecone retrieval. The five have to be visibly distinct so the reader doesn't feel they're reading variations on one story.

- **Chokepoint 1 — EUV lithography and the ASML constraint.** The Netherlands as the single point of failure for advanced semiconductor manufacturing globally, and what Pax Silica export coordination means for Europe's own access. *Primary domain: Supply Chain. Secondary: Policy.*
- **Chokepoint 2 — Helium and the noble-gas supply for fab operations.** A live, underreported story (already covered in the Helium Scarcity article). The chokepoint with the shortest historical precedent and the longest tail. *Primary domain: Supply Chain.*
- **Chokepoint 3 — Critical minerals and the Greenland scramble.** Tying European industrial sovereignty to Arctic geopolitics. *Primary domain: Supply Chain. Secondary: Policy.*
- **Chokepoint 4 — German automotive AI compliance under the Act.** The largest single category of European exposure when AI Act enforcement starts. Where regulatory chokepoint becomes industrial chokepoint. *Primary domain: Policy. Secondary: Talent.*
- **Chokepoint 5 — Senior process-engineer flow out of European fabs.** The Talent chokepoint the others don't see. The fabs may be built; the people to run them at yield may already be leaving. *Primary domain: Talent. Secondary: Supply Chain.*

The five together hit all three forensic domains at least twice and apply both methods (Long-Memory and Scenario) at least twice. The full Methodology Audit on the front cover should show all six cells lit.

### Section 7 — The Pattern Across the Five (1.5 pages)

- All five are visible *from the edge* before they're visible from the centre — the framing pivot back to the brand

- All five reward early monitoring more than they reward post-hoc commentary

## Visual design notes

Not a design spec — these are notes for whoever produces the actual file.

- **Type system**: same families as the website. Serif for body, mono for the Methodology Audit cells, sans for the Stone Truth callouts.
- **Colour**: silicon-amber for accents, stone-teal for the Methodology Audit cells, slate-deep backgrounds on full-bleed pages. Match the site exactly so the PDF reads as part of the same family.
- **Methodology Audit visualisation**: the 3×2 grid appears at the top of each chokepoint section as small filled / unfilled cells, like a status indicator. Reader learns the visual grammar within two sections.
- **Stone Truths**: every Stone Truth gets the same treatment — italic, 1.4× line-height, silicon-amber left border, distinct from body. Same as the website's briefing-card style.
- **Charts**: at most one per chokepoint section. If a section needs more than one chart it should probably be its own paid Intelligence Series PDF.
- **Print-readable**: assume some readers will print. 11pt body, 14pt section heads, generous gutters.

## What this PDF deliberately does not do

To keep the scope tight and to leave headroom for the paid Intelligence Series:

- **No deep modelling.** Value at Stake numbers are headline-grade ranges, not the full quantitative work. The paid PDFs go deeper.
- **No vendor or product recommendations.** This is analysis, not procurement advice.
- **No embedded political position.** The Atlantic Drift is described forensically, not from a US-aligned or EU-aligned posture.
- **No predictions with single-point confidence.** Three scenarios per chokepoint. The reader picks.

If a section drifts into any of the above, it gets cut to keep the document under 15 pages and to keep the paid product distinct.
