## Content Types

Each piece of content is classified into one of three formats:

### Signal (Breaking Analysis)
- **Purpose:** Rapid response to breaking developments
- **Length:** 800–1,500 words
- **Turnaround:** 24–72 hours from event
- **Style:** Direct, actionable implications highlighted upfront
- **Example Topics:**
  - New AI Act guidance from European Commission
  - Export control expansion announcements
  - Major semiconductor capacity investment decisions

### Deep Dive (Long-Form Analysis)
- **Purpose:** Comprehensive examination of complex issues
- **Length:** 3,000–6,000 words
- **Research:** Multi-source investigation with primary documentation
- **Style:** Structured sections with clear navigation; data visualizations encouraged
- **Example Topics:**
  - Full AI Act compliance roadmap by sector
  - Comparative analysis of EU vs. US AI governance
  - Semiconductor supply chain risk assessment frameworks

### Tool Guide
- **Purpose:** Instructions and context for interactive tools
- **Length:** Variable (500–2,000 words)
- **Integration:** Links to and explains corresponding tool features
- **Style:** Task-oriented, step-by-step where appropriate
- **Example Topics:**
  - How to use the Compliance Checker for AI Act risk classification
  - Interpreting Supply Chain Mapper results

## Content Quality Standards

Every piece of content should meet these criteria:

- [ ] **Actionable**: Readers leave knowing what to do or decide
- [ ] **Substantiated**: Claims linked to primary sources where possible
- [ ] **Persona-Tagged**: Explicitly relevant to at least one decision-maker type
- [ ] **Timely**: Dated content marked; evergreen content identified
- [ ] **Interconnected**: Links to related analysis and tools where relevant
- [ ] **Accessible**: Complex topics explained without jargon barriers
