**The Honest Assessment**

Before laying out the strategy, it is worth being direct about what
£7,000--£10,000 per month actually requires. That is £84,000--£120,000
per year --- a substantial income that many full-time content creators
never reach, and most YouTube channels never generate. However, your
situation has several characteristics that make this target more
achievable than it would be for a typical creator.

First, your niche is high-value. You are not competing for the attention
of teenagers; you are serving senior professionals with real budgets and
genuine problems. The commercial value of your audience per person is
many multiples higher than a mainstream channel.

Second, you have genuine, deep expertise. Thirty years in the technology
industry is not something that can be faked or replicated by a younger
creator with a webcam and enthusiasm. This credibility is the foundation
everything else is built on.

Third, your target audience has a habit of paying for expertise. Senior
professionals routinely pay for industry reports, advisory services,
conferences, and professional development. The cultural barrier to
payment is much lower than in consumer markets.

Fourth, your content has a natural product ladder. The progression from
free YouTube video to paid briefing to advisory engagement feels logical
and proportionate, not forced.

The critical insight is this: £7,000--£10,000 per month will not come
from YouTube alone. It will not come from any single revenue stream. It
will come from a deliberately constructed portfolio of income sources,
with YouTube as the credibility engine at the top and consulting or
advisory work as the highest-value component. The strategy below is
designed to build all of these in parallel, starting revenue generation
early rather than waiting until the YouTube audience is large.

**Stream 1: Consulting and Advisory (£3,000--£4,000/month)**

This is the fastest path to significant revenue and should be treated as
the primary income driver, at least in the first 12--18 months. Your 30
years of experience is the asset here --- the YouTube channel simply
makes it visible to people who would otherwise never find you.

**Product Menu**

**AI Readiness Assessment (£750--£1,500)**

A structured half-day engagement where you assess a European SME's
current AI usage, dependency on US platforms, data sovereignty exposure,
and readiness for AI Act compliance. You deliver a concise written
report (8--12 pages) with a traffic-light risk matrix and three priority
recommendations. This is your entry-level consulting product. It is
priced low enough to be an easy decision for a mid-sized company, but
high enough to be sustainable for you. One of these per month at £1,000
average is £12,000 per year.

**Strategic Briefing (£1,500--£2,500)**

A two-to-three-hour private briefing for a leadership team or board on a
specific topic. You prepare a tailored presentation and lead a
discussion. Topics might include: the practical implications of the AI
Act for their sector, how to evaluate and reduce US cloud dependency, or
a geopolitical risk briefing on semiconductor supply chains relevant to
their industry. These sessions are high-impact and high-margin. Two per
month at £2,000 average is £4,000.

**Monthly Advisory Retainer (£2,000--£3,500/month)**

An ongoing relationship with an organisation that needs regular access
to your analysis and perspective. Typically four to six hours per month,
including a monthly written briefing, ad-hoc questions by email, and a
monthly call. This is the most valuable product because it creates
predictable recurring revenue. Even one retainer client at £2,500/month
transforms your income stability. Two clients is £5,000/month from this
stream alone.

**How to Generate Consulting Revenue Quickly**

The mistake most content creators make is waiting until they have a
large audience before offering consulting. You should be offering
advisory services from month one, because your credibility comes from
your career, not your subscriber count. The YouTube channel accelerates
enquiries, but it is not a prerequisite.

1.  **Leverage your existing network immediately.** You have 30 years of
    professional contacts. Announce your new positioning to your network
    through LinkedIn, personal emails, and any industry groups you
    belong to. Frame it as: "After 30 years in the engine room, I'm now
    advising European businesses on navigating AI regulation and digital
    sovereignty. Here's what I can help with." This is likely to
    generate your first clients within weeks, not months.

2.  **Use the YouTube channel as a portfolio.** Every video you publish
    is a demonstration of your analytical capability. When you pitch a
    strategic briefing to a potential client, you can point them to
    specific videos that demonstrate your expertise on topics relevant
    to their business.

3.  **Offer the AI Readiness Assessment at a launch price.** For your
    first five clients, offer the assessment at £500 (discounted from
    £1,000) in exchange for a testimonial and a case study you can
    reference. This builds your consulting portfolio rapidly.

4.  **Speak at industry events and webinars.** Even small, free speaking
    engagements put you in front of potential advisory clients. Offer a
    30-minute talk on "What the AI Act Means for Your Business" to local
    business groups, trade associations, or online industry communities.

**Stream 2: Digital Products (£2,000--£3,000/month)**

Digital products are where you achieve scale without trading time for
money. You create them once, update them periodically, and sell them
repeatedly. For your niche, the most effective products are those that
save your audience time on complex problems they already know they need
to solve. The key principle is this: your free YouTube content explains
why something matters; your digital products provide the tools and
frameworks to actually do something about it.

**Product 1: The AI Act Compliance Toolkit (£49--£99)**

This is your flagship digital product and should be one of the first
things you build, ideally before or alongside your YouTube launch. The
AI Act enforcement deadline in August 2026 creates genuine urgency.
Every European business using AI needs to understand their obligations,
and most have not started.

**What it contains:**

- **Risk classification decision tree:** A visual flowchart that helps a
  non-specialist determine whether their AI systems fall into the
  prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, or minimal-risk categories under
  the AI Act. This alone is worth the price for many businesses.

- **Compliance checklist by risk category:** A detailed, action-oriented
  checklist for each risk category. What documentation do they need?
  What assessments must they conduct? What records must they keep?

- **Template letters and policies:** Draft internal AI governance
  policies, AI system registration templates, and transparency notice
  templates that a business can adapt to their own context.

- **Timeline and deadline tracker:** A clear visual timeline showing
  when different AI Act provisions come into force and what must be in
  place by each date.

- **Quarterly update service:** Purchasers receive quarterly email
  updates as new guidance is published or enforcement actions occur.
  This turns a one-off purchase into an ongoing relationship.

**Format:** PDF document (40--60 pages) with accompanying spreadsheet
templates. Delivered via Gumroad, Payhip, or your own website.

**Revenue potential:** At £79 average price point, 20 sales per month is
£1,580/month. This is achievable once you have a YouTube audience of
3,000--5,000 subscribers with regular content referencing AI Act
compliance.

**Product 2: Sector-Specific AI Briefings (£29--£49 each)**

A series of focused PDF briefings, each addressing a specific sector's
AI landscape. These are shorter than the Compliance Toolkit (15--25
pages each) and designed to be read in a single sitting by a busy
executive.

**Initial titles:**

- **\"AI and European Manufacturing: The 2026 Landscape\"** --- for
  operations directors and supply chain managers in manufacturing.

- **\"AI in European Financial Services: Compliance and Opportunity\"**
  --- for compliance officers and strategy directors in banking and
  insurance.

- **\"AI for Professional Services: What Law Firms, Consultancies, and
  Accountancies Need to Know\"** --- for managing partners and
  technology leads.

- **\"AI and the Public Sector: A Practical Guide for European
  Government\"** --- for policy advisors and digital transformation
  leads.

Each briefing takes approximately two to three days to write well.
Release one per month as part of your content calendar. At £39 average
price with 30 sales per month across the series, this generates
£1,170/month once you have four or five titles in the catalogue.

**Product 3: The AI Audit Checklist Pack (£19--£29)**

A lower-priced product designed to capture buyers who are not yet ready
to invest in the full Compliance Toolkit. This is a set of structured
checklists that a business can use to quickly audit their AI usage and
exposure.

**What it contains:**

- **AI systems inventory template:** A spreadsheet for cataloguing every
  AI tool and system in use across the organisation, with fields for
  vendor, data flows, risk category, and contract status.

- **Vendor dependency scorecard:** A scoring matrix for assessing how
  dependent the organisation is on specific US AI providers, and the
  switching cost for each.

- **Quick compliance gap analysis:** A 20-question self-assessment that
  identifies the most urgent compliance gaps relative to the AI Act.

**Revenue potential:** This is a volume product. At £24 average price,
40 sales per month is £960/month. It also serves as a gateway to the
more expensive Compliance Toolkit --- buyers who complete the audit and
discover gaps will naturally want the detailed toolkit to address them.

**Product 4: "AI for Grown-Ups" Course (£149--£299)**

A self-paced video course that extends your YouTube content into a
structured learning experience. This is a larger undertaking and should
be a month six to nine project, once you have refined your teaching
approach through YouTube and have audience feedback on what resonates.

**Structure:** Eight to twelve modules, each 15--20 minutes of video
with accompanying worksheets. Covers: what AI actually is (without the
hype), how to evaluate AI tools for your business, hands-on setup of
practical AI assistants, understanding the regulatory landscape, and
building an AI strategy for a European business.

**Platform:** Teachable, Podia, or Kajabi. All handle hosting, payment,
and delivery. Teachable takes the smallest cut.

**Revenue potential:** At £199 price point, even 10 sales per month is
£1,990/month. Course sales tend to be spiky --- they surge when you
promote them on YouTube and in the newsletter, then taper between
promotions. An evergreen funnel (automated email sequence driving to the
course) smooths this out over time.

**Stream 3: Paid Newsletter and Membership (£1,500--£2,000/month)**

Your newsletter is the bridge between free content and paid products. It
builds the deepest relationship with your audience and creates
predictable monthly recurring revenue. The model is straightforward: a
free weekly edition available to anyone, with a premium tier that
delivers substantially more depth.

**Free Tier (Substack or Ghost)**

A weekly email that complements your YouTube content. Where the video
tells the story, the newsletter provides the data, the source links, and
the detailed implications. It should feel like the analytical companion
to the video, not a transcript of it. This is your primary list-building
tool and the mechanism for moving YouTube viewers into a direct
relationship that you own and control.

**Premium Tier (£15--£25/month or £150--£250/year)**

**What premium subscribers get:**

- **Weekly premium briefing:** A longer, more detailed analysis that
  goes beyond what you publish on YouTube. This is where your
  methodology frameworks operate at full depth. Think of it as the
  intelligence briefing a senior executive would want on their desk
  every Monday morning.

- **Monthly scenario assessment:** A structured one-page assessment of a
  specific risk or opportunity, with three scenarios and probability
  estimates. For example: "If the US expands semiconductor export
  controls to cover AI model weights, what happens to European
  businesses using American AI platforms?"

- **Early access to digital products:** Premium subscribers get your
  briefing PDFs and toolkit updates before they go on general sale, and
  at a discounted price.

- **Quarterly live Q&A:** A 60-minute group video call where premium
  subscribers can ask questions directly. This builds community and
  gives you invaluable insight into what your audience actually needs.

**Pricing strategy:** Offer both monthly and annual options. Annual
pricing should offer a meaningful discount (equivalent to two months
free) to encourage commitment and reduce churn. At £20/month, 75--100
paid subscribers generates £1,500--£2,000/month. This is achievable
within 12 months if you consistently deliver premium-quality analysis.

**Conversion benchmarks:** In high-quality niche newsletters, 5--10% of
free subscribers convert to paid. If 12--15% of your YouTube subscribers
join the free newsletter, and 7% of those convert to paid, you need
approximately 7,000--15,000 YouTube subscribers to reach 75--100 paid
newsletter subscribers. This is a 12--18 month trajectory, but the
revenue compounds as the back catalogue of premium content grows and
makes the subscription more valuable over time.

**Streams 4 and 5: Sponsorship and YouTube Ads (£1,000--£2,000/month
combined)**

These are covered in detail in the previous document and the sponsorship
discussion. To summarise: YouTube ad revenue provides a small but
growing baseline (£250--£500/month at 5,000--10,000 subscribers in your
high-CPM niche), while sponsorship becomes meaningful once you pass
3,000--5,000 subscribers (£750--£1,500 per integration). Together, these
streams cover your production costs and contribute to the monthly total
without requiring you to build or maintain anything beyond your regular
content.

The important principle is to never depend on these streams. They are
bonuses that improve the overall economics, not foundations you build
on.

**Getting Up and Running Quickly Without Compromising Quality**

You asked specifically about speed. Here is what I would prioritise to
generate revenue as early as possible while maintaining the quality
standard that your credibility depends on.

**Week 1--2: Build Before You Launch**

1.  **Create the AI Audit Checklist Pack.** This is your simplest
    digital product --- three to four spreadsheet templates and a short
    PDF guide. You can build this in two to three days. Price it at £24.
    Set it up on Gumroad or Payhip. This gives you a product to sell
    from day one.

2.  **Draft the AI Act Compliance Toolkit outline.** Begin the larger
    product. You do not need it complete before launch, but having the
    structure clear allows you to reference it in early content as
    "coming soon."

3.  **Write your consulting services page.** Even a simple one-page
    document describing your three advisory offerings (Assessment,
    Briefing, Retainer) with pricing. You need this to send to potential
    clients.

4.  **Announce to your network.** Before the YouTube channel launches,
    tell your professional contacts what you are doing. LinkedIn posts,
    direct emails, industry forum posts. Frame it as: "I'm launching
    something new --- here's what I've learned in 30 years and why it
    matters now."

**Week 3--4: Launch and Sell Simultaneously**

1.  **Launch the YouTube channel with Week 1 content from the
    calendar.** Your origin story video. Film it, publish it, and begin
    the weekly rhythm.

2.  **Launch the free newsletter on the same day.** Even if the first
    issue is simply a written version of your launch announcement plus a
    preview of what subscribers can expect.

3.  **List the Checklist Pack for sale.** Mention it naturally in your
    first newsletter and in the YouTube video description. Not as a hard
    sell, but as a resource: "If you want to audit your own AI exposure,
    I've put together a checklist pack."

4.  **Send your first consulting outreach.** Contact five to ten people
    in your network who lead businesses that would benefit from an AI
    Readiness Assessment. Offer the launch price.

**Month 2--3: Compound and Complete**

1.  **Complete and launch the AI Act Compliance Toolkit.** This is your
    premium digital product and should be thoroughly well-made. It is
    the product that establishes your reputation for quality.

2.  **Publish your first sector-specific briefing.** Choose the sector
    you know best. The first one will take longest; subsequent ones
    follow the same template.

3.  **Continue weekly YouTube content.** The content library is growing.
    Each video is a potential entry point for new viewers and a
    reference for product sales.

4.  **Collect and publish testimonials from early consulting clients.**
    Social proof accelerates everything else.

**Quality Guardrails**

Speed and quality are only in conflict if you try to do everything at
once. The strategy above deliberately sequences your work so that you
build simpler products first (Checklist Pack) while the more complex
ones (Compliance Toolkit, Course) develop in the background. Here are
the non-negotiable quality standards:

1.  **Never publish a digital product you would not be comfortable
    presenting to a room of senior executives.** Your audience will
    judge you on the first product they buy. If it is sloppy, they will
    never buy another.

2.  **YouTube videos do not need to be polished, but they must be
    well-structured.** A simple video filmed on your phone with clear
    thinking and useful content will outperform a glossy production with
    shallow analysis. Invest your time in research and scripting, not in
    visual effects.

3.  **The newsletter must be genuinely valuable every single week.** If
    you do not have something worth saying, write a shorter issue rather
    than padding it. Your readers are busy professionals who will
    unsubscribe the moment they feel their time is being wasted.

4.  **Consulting deliverables must be impeccable.** These are the
    highest-value interactions and the primary source of referrals. A
    well-written assessment report that genuinely helps a client will
    generate two to three referrals.

**Summary: Is £7,000--£10,000 Per Month Realistic?**

Yes, but with important caveats.

It is realistic because your niche is commercially valuable, your
expertise is genuine and deep, your target audience is accustomed to
paying for professional insight, and the product ladder from free
content to premium consulting is natural and proportionate.

It requires 12 to 18 months of consistent, high-quality output across
multiple channels. It requires treating this as a proper business from
day one, not a side project. It requires building and maintaining five
revenue streams simultaneously, each reinforcing the others. And it
requires the discipline to stay consistent through the early months when
the audience is small and the revenue is modest.

The most important strategic decision is to start generating consulting
revenue immediately through your existing network, rather than waiting
for YouTube to deliver an audience. The channel is an accelerant, not
the engine. Your 30 years of experience, your analytical perspective,
and your genuine independence are the engine. Everything else is
packaging and distribution.

The second most important decision is to build digital products early
and sell them from the start. The AI Act Compliance Toolkit and Audit
Checklist Pack address urgent, specific problems that your audience
already knows they have. These products sell themselves once they are
visible to the right people, and every YouTube video and newsletter
edition makes them more visible.

If you execute consistently, the trajectory from £1,000--£2,500 in month
three to £7,000--£10,000 by month twelve to eighteen is achievable. It
will not be linear --- some months will be strong (a new retainer
client, a course launch promotion), others will be quieter. But the
compounding effect of a growing content library, an expanding product
catalogue, and an increasingly visible personal brand creates momentum
that is very difficult to stop once it is established.
